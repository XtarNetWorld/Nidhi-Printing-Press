How we work

From idea to finished print in four simple steps.

  1. Tell us what you need

    Visit the shop or message on WhatsApp with size, quantity and paper choice. A photo or rough sketch is enough.

  2. Design & proof

    We prepare the layout or use your artwork and share a proof for your approval before printing.

  3. Press & finishing

    Printing, cutting, binding, lamination or numbering, handled with care and quality checks.

  4. Pick up or delivery

    Collect from the shop, or ask us about delivery to your area.

Good to know

Questions we hear often.

How do I place an order?

Walk into the shop, call, or message us on WhatsApp with the job type, size, quantity and when you need it. We confirm the rate and share a proof before printing.

Can you design it for me?

Yes. Share your matter, logo and a reference if you have one. We prepare the layout in Hindi, English or both and show you a proof for approval.

Which file formats can I send?

PDF, CorelDRAW (CDR), Photoshop (PSD), JPG and PNG all work. If you only have a photo or a rough sketch, send that and we can rebuild it.

How long does printing take?

It depends on the job, paper and finishing. Simple jobs are quick, wedding cards and large orders take longer. Tell us your date and we will confirm if it is possible.

Do you give better rates for bulk or regular orders?

Yes. Ask us for a bulk or repeat-order rate when you share your requirement.

Do you deliver?

Ask us on WhatsApp about delivery for your location and order size.
